Godot 4 editable planar rig

Open project.godot and run to preview with an automatically fitted camera.
For an existing game, instantiate rig.tscn. Its glTF child contains native
Skeleton3D bones, skinned MeshInstance3D parts and AnimationPlayer tracks.
Use an orthographic Camera3D looking toward the XY plane to place it in a game.
This is a planar 3D rig; the existing sprite export remains the native 2D option.

The wrapper applies visibility/opacity and audio while playing the native clip.
set_clothing(group_id, variant_id) changes one category; empty variant removes it.
set_part_color(part_node_name, Color(...)) independently tints a garment or body part.
Find IDs, names, variants and mesh membership in rig.json. Stop AnimationPlayer
before manually posing bones. Native bones, weights, meshes and materials remain
editable in an inherited glTF scene. FFD/constraints were sampled at export FPS.
